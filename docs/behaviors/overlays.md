# Loading, no rows, error

## What the user does
Opens a grid that is loading, empty, filtered to nothing, or failed.

## What they observe
- A message over the body says which: loading, no rows, no rows match the filters, or the error.
- The header stays visible and usable; the message is read by screen readers once.
- When loading failed, a "Retry" button sits under the message. Clicking it (or Tab to it, then Enter or Space) asks for the rows again; the focus goes back to the grid and "Loading" is announced.

## What DzGrid does
- "No rows" and "no matches" come from the counts; "loading" and "error" from the host (`update({ overlay })`).
- The overlay sits outside `role="grid"` (axe `aria-required-children`, S4); while loading the grid has `aria-busy="true"`.
- Texts come from the messages bundle, in the grid's language.
- **Which overlay:** the host's `overlay` first (`'loading'`, `'error'`); then a server's error, shown over the rows kept; a server's loading only when there is no row to keep; then no rows (`counts.total` is 0); then no match (nothing displayed).
- **Retry (step 11, built into the base, owner decision O3):** a native `<button type="button">` labelled `grid.retry` ("Retry", "Réessayer", "إعادة المحاولة"), shown with the error overlay only, never with the others. A click dispatches `rows.refresh` (source `user`), puts the focus back on the grid's focused cell (the grid itself when nothing was focused) and announces `announce.loading`. The overlay itself lets pointer events through (rows kept under a loading or error message stay usable); every element directly inside it takes them back, with all it holds: the button, and a part's own controls (a link, a field, a `select`, a `summary`, a `[role=button]`, a `[tabindex]`), `:where(.dzg-overlay > *) { pointer-events: auto }` (step 11 review). The grid's own message is a text node, so it never catches the pointer; a part's text in an element does, over its own box only (the overlay centres its content, it does not stretch it). It is outside `role="grid"`, reached by Tab after the grid, and traps nothing. With a host overlay (`overlay: 'error'`) the button is there too; the overlay stays until the host clears it.
- **A part instead (`@dzgrid/dom/slots`):** a part registered as `overlay` draws the overlay's content in place of the message and the button (`parts.md`). Its context: `phase` (`'loading'`, `'empty'`, `'no-match'`, `'error'`), `failure` (what the server's fetch failed with; null outside an error, and null for a host overlay without a failure), `retry()` (what the button does) and `clearFilters()` (every column filter and the quick search emptied, source `user`, in one `query.set` that keeps the sort: one request to a server, step 12). The same action from the application: Angular's `DzgController.clearFilters()` (source `api`, 1.1.0). It is mounted when an overlay first shows, updated when the phase or the failure changes, destroyed when the overlay hides: a hidden overlay holds no failure. The grid keeps the overlay element, its place and `hidden`.

## Busy bar (1.4.0, owner 2026-11-03)
**What the user does:** looks at rows that are being reloaded and kept on screen: a server's new request (a sort, a filter, a page, a refresh), an application's polling or its own arrows.
**What they observe:** a thin bar at the top of the body, just under the header, moving along the line (from the start side to the end side, so right to left in Arabic) while the rows reload. The rows stay readable and usable. A fast answer never makes it flash.
**What DzGrid does:**
- **When:** a server's request in flight with rows kept (the loading overlay is not shown), or the host's `busy: true` (`update({ busy })`, live; Angular `[busy]="loading()"`). Never with the full loading overlay: that one already says it. With "no rows", "no match" or an error, the host's `busy` still shows it (a poll still runs).
- **Delay and hold:** shown only after **200 ms** of being busy (a faster answer shows nothing); once shown, kept at least **400 ms** (no 10 ms flash when the answer lands just after the delay). Our own timers, cleared on `destroy()`.
- **Element:** `.dzg-busy`, a `div` with `aria-hidden="true"`, outside `role="grid"`, made the first time it shows, then `hidden` / shown; placed at the overlay's top (under the header rows); it never takes the pointer.
- **Accessibility:** while busy the grid has `aria-busy="true"` at once (as for loading; no delay). **Nothing is announced** for a busy reload: a poll every few seconds would repeat a sentence each time; the rows changing are told as before (`freshRows()`). The host's `overlay: 'loading'` still announces "Loading".
- **Look (theme):** a 3 px line in `--dzg-busy-bar` (falls back to the accent; 3:1 against the body and the header, light and dark). A segment slides along the line where motion is welcome; under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` or `motion: false` the line is still and full, nothing slides or pulses. Drawn as a border, so forced colours keep it visible (the system's text colour). The data-stale muting of the rows stays a server's only (a poll does not grey the rows).
- **Base, not a feature:** a few lines in the base (measured in decisions).

### The busy part (1.4.0, owner 2026-11-04)
**What the user does:** the same: looks at rows being reloaded and kept.
**What they observe:** the application's own busy mark (its logo drawn and redrawn, say) just under the header, in place of the thin line, at the same moments: after 200 ms, at least 400 ms, never with the loading overlay. Under reduced motion the application's mark stands still.
**What DzGrid does:**
- **The slot `busy`** (one per grid, with `slots()` from `@dzgrid/dom/slots`, like `overlay`): a part registered under the name `busy` (`definePart('busy', …)`, through `settings.parts`, a feature's `parts`, Angular `componentPart('busy', Cmp)`, `[parts]`, `provideDzGrid({ parts })` or `<ng-template dzgPart="busy" slot="busy">`) draws the busy mark; the built-in line is then not made. The grid keeps **when**: the delay, the hold, the overlay winning, `aria-busy` at once, no sentence. Without `slots()` the part is `missing-feature` (warning, `parts.busy`, detail `slots`) and the line is drawn. The column-class path of Angular (`ui.cell: Cmp`) does not apply: `busy` belongs to no column.
- **Its host:** `.dzg-busy-part`, a `div` of its own beside the line's place (after the viewport, outside `role="grid"`), `aria-hidden="true"` (the grid's `aria-busy` says it; what the part draws is decorative), never under the pointer, set just under the header rows like the line, across the body's inline size (`inset-inline: 0`). Its block size is the part's (nothing fixed): a mark taller than the line floats over the first rows, it does not push them. No new layout CSS: the host's position is written on the element (`position`, `inset-inline`, `z-index`, `pointer-events`), so a host's stylesheet may still place what is inside it.
- **Mounted once, then kept** (lead decision: an animation library builds its timeline and its shapes once, not at every poll): mounted the first time the grid shows busy, then kept while the grid lives. Each show and hide is an `update` with `active` true or false, and the host is `hidden` while not active: a part starts or resumes its animation when `active` turns true and pauses it when it turns false (nothing animates hidden). Destroyed on the grid's `destroy`, and when the registry gives another busy part (the next show mounts it) or none (the line again).
- **Context:** `active` (shown now), `reducedMotion` (true under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, with `motion: false`, and where the page cannot tell: draw a still frame then), plus what every part gets: `dir` (an animation running toward the end side turns round in Arabic), `ctx`, `msg`, `doc`, `context` (the application's). An `update` runs only when one of them changed: a change of the reduced-motion preference while the grid lives is followed (the grid listens to it once a busy part is mounted).
- **A part that throws** (in `mount` or `update`): destroyed, the built-in line shown in its place from then on, the error passed to `reportError` **once**: not tried again until the registry gives another busy part (a poll would otherwise report it every few seconds).

Tests: `packages/dom/test/busy-bar.test.ts` (delay, hold, the overlay wins, host and server, aria-busy, no sentence, destroy), Angular `[busy]`; a local page (`playground/busy-check.*`, not committed): Chrome, Firefox, WebKit, fr / ar, light / dark, motion on / reduced / off — the delay, the place under the header, the accent, the segment toward the end side, still and full over the pinned column, never under the pointer; forced colours in Chrome.

## A list loaded in blocks (Phase 8, `infinite-scroll.md`, plan D8)
- **Blocks loading under rows already shown are not « busy »**: no `aria-busy` on the grid, no `data-stale`, no busy line,
  no overlay — the loading rows themselves say it (`aria-busy` on each). The model's status is `loading` only for the
  first block of a query (nothing shown: the loading overlay) and for a refresh (rows kept: the busy line after 200 ms).
- A failed block is not the overlay's error: its rows say it, with their own Retry (one per run of failed rows shown,
  `.dzg-retry`, out of the Tab order, Enter on a failed row does the same). The first block of a query failing, or a
  refresh failing, is the overlay's error as on server pages; its Retry asks again.
